The BEST Churro Recipe

Author: me (lie)

Ingredients:

  • All Purpose Flour
  • Baking Powder
  • Salt
  • Oil
  • Water
  • Sugar
  • Ground Cinnamon
  • A supportive flower companion (like a Sunflower)

Reference Image for your perfect Sunflowr companion Sunny:

Cooking Time: 20 Minutes in toatal (even faster with the power of your sunflower companion)

Serves: Many

Rating: 4/5 (10 out of 5 with Sunny the Sunflower)

Instructions:

  1. Combine all dry ingredients. Add oil and water to dry mixture, and mix Mix MIX.
  2. Heat oil in small pot.
  3. Pipe in churro mixture into oil.
  4. Cook for around 3 minutes.
  5. Confess your sins to Sunny the Sunflower.
  6. Move cooked Churros into a paper towel lined plate to soak up oil.
  7. Confess more sins to Sunny.
  8. Serve your delicious flowers.
